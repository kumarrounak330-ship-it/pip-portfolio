How I Got Here

Ek baar, Midnight Nursery ke upar ek chhota sa shooting star ek mulayam dream-cloud mein ulajh gaya. Jab taaron ki dhool hati, toh wahan Pip tha—ek curious sa baby jisne pyari si nightcap pehni thi!

Pip ko akelepan ki shanti bilkul pasand nahi thi. Isliye usne chamakte hue moonbeams jama karke glowing lanterns aur magical music boxes banana shuru kar diya. Uski pyari giggles sun kar nursery ke dare hue chhote creatures ko bahut sukoon milta tha.

Pip ka yeh magic dekh kar, use "Junior Dream Engineer" ka title de diya gaya. Ab Pip apni har raat pyare sapne aur nightlights banane mein bitata hai, taaki koi bhi andhere mein akele na dare!
